# Product Source of Truth

## What Crado is
AI-native regulatory engineering. Crado connects product design, regulatory requirements and real test evidence so hardware teams can move regulatory feedback earlier into engineering.

## Initial user
Hardware/electronics engineer working on connected electronic products, especially teams without enough dedicated EMC expertise.

## Initial job
After a radiated-emissions pre-compliance or formal test exposes a problem, help the engineer reduce the diagnostic search space, record the investigation, compare the engineering change against a new measurement, and maintain the evidence relationship to the product revision.

## Magic moment
The engineer enters a failed frequency/measurement and product context. Crado identifies plausible product-frequency relationships, produces evidence-grounded hypotheses, asks a targeted question if necessary, and updates the investigation when new observations arrive.

## Long-term loop
Design → requirements → measurement → failure intelligence → engineering change → re-measurement → Regulatory State.

## Non-goals
Crado does not certify a product, replace a lab, replace an EMC engineer, guarantee a pass, or pretend an LLM is an electromagnetic simulator.
